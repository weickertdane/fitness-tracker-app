Privacy Policy for fitness tracker app

Last Updated: October 22, 2025

Overview

This fitness tracker app is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The fitness tracker app operates entirely on your device. All workout data, preferences, and settings remain stored locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone. We have no access to your information.

Health Data

The fitness tracker app may access HealthKit data (such as heart rate, calories burned, and workout metrics) solely for display within the app. This data:

  • Remains on your device
  • Is never transmitted to external servers
  • Is never shared with third parties
  • Is controlled by your iOS privacy settings

Third-Party Services

The fitness tracker app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is suitable for all ages and maintains the same privacy practices regardless of the user's age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store review section or by creating an issue on our GitHub repository.
